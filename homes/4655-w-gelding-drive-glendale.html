About this home

TWO PRIMARY SUITES WITH SEPARATE ENTRANCES, NEARLY HALF AN ACRE & NO HOA! This 4-bedroom, 3-bath home sits on a spacious corner lot offering privacy, versatility, and room to spread out. Ideal for multigenerational living or guests, the dual primary suites provide flexible living options. The tri-level, open-concept floor plan features classic wood flooring, a striking wraparound fireplace, French doors, and a charming entryway with brick flower shelving. The gourmet kitchen boasts granite countertops and a stone tile backsplash. Outside, enjoy mature palm trees, an expansive backyard, and an oversized covered patio.
